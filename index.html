♡A letter from me to you♡

Hello, Megha ❤️

I love you, baby. ❤️ I wanted to start this by saying that, because sometimes I wish I could express it better. I wish I could show you just how much I love you, so you would never have to wonder about it, not even for a second. 🫂🤍

Those pretty eyes of yours… 🥹❤️ And when the sunlight falls on them? Ohhhhh my God, baby! ☀️ I could literally drown in them. Your beautiful, innocent face, your cute smile, and that long hair of yours… I just want to play with it, run my fingers through it, and give you the sweetest head massage. 🫶🤍

But more than anything, I love your beautiful soul. ❤️‍🩹 The way you care for me and your family, even when you already have so much on your plate. Housework, your job, all the things you have to handle, and barely any time left for yourself — still, you somehow make time and keep doing so much for everyone. 🥹 You know, baby, sometimes I literally feel like God must be a woman, because LOOK AT YOU. ❤️

You know how much I overthink. 🥺 No matter what anyone says, my mind sometimes just refuses to stop. But do you know what works? Being around you, baby. Just being with you. 🫂🤍 That's all it takes for me to feel calm, breathe a little easier, and forget about everything else for a while. You have that effect on me without even trying. ❤️

And about your family, babu… 🫂 I know I don't know everything about what goes on at home, and I don't want to assume I understand it all. But it hurts me to know that there have been times when you've gone to sleep without eating because nobody asked you. 🥺 Baby, I want you to remember that you deserve to be cared for, too. Sometimes people get tired, distracted, and caught up in everything going on in their own minds. Maybe your mother forgets in those moments, it doesn't mean she doesn't love you. I hope you can be gentle with her, and with yourself, too. 🤍

And if nobody asks, I want to be the one who asks you, every time: “Aapne khana khaya, Baby. Kya khaya?” 🥹❤️ Please don't go to sleep hungry, okay? Promise me you'll try to take care of yourself. 🫂 It makes me sad knowing you haven't eaten. I wish I could be there beside you, make you sit down, and feed you with my own hands. 🤍

I love you, baby. I love you so, so much. ❤️‍🩹 And I won't lie — thinking about a future where we may not get to be together the way we want to is painful. 💔 Sometimes that thought brings me to my knees. I can't imagine not having you in my everyday life. I know there are things we may not be able to change, and that hurts more than I know how to explain. 🥺 I will miss you in so many little moments, in the ordinary days, and in all the things I wish we could have shared. You are one of the best things that has ever happened to me, and I love you, meri jaan. ❤️🫂

I wanted to do so many little things with you. 🥹 Paint your nails, put oil in your hair, give you massages, dance with you, watch the stars, try different kinds of food, travel to new places, and make silly memories that only we would understand. 🤍 The list honestly never ends. It's not even about doing something extraordinary. I just wanted those ordinary moments with you, the kind that become special simply because it's you and me. ❤️

And baby, whatever life looks like and wherever we are, please remember that you can count on me. 🫂❤️ You can call me anytime and talk to me about anything. You don't have to think twice before dialing my number. Whether you're happy, upset, confused, tired, or just need someone to listen, I'm here. You don't have to carry every single thing by yourself. 🤍

I love you, Megha. ❤️ For your eyes, your smile, your heart, your little habits, your strength, and all the tiny things that make you you. I wish life could give us every moment we've dreamed of. Even with the sadness I carry about the future, I'm grateful that I got to love you and know a person like you. 🥹❤️‍🩹

Always with love,
your kuchupuchu 🫂❤️
